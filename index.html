Ready to bring your music into the command line?

Install the package globally via npm.

$ npm install -g mascii

MASCII was born out of a personal challenge: crafting a dedicated audio environment tailored entirely for developer focus. It strips away heavy electron frameworks, graphical overhead, and clutter—giving you lightweight streaming performance right in your current working directory.

By tapping the 'y' key, you gain immediate access to online audio streams and YouTube content, eliminating browser tab distractions so you can remain anchored in your development workspace.

This project is shared fully transparently: I am not a professional programmer, and this tool represents a continuous exploration of stable milestone architecture.

Because it is built entirely on community framework logic, optimization is ongoing. Glitches are welcome indicators of progress, and all architectural feedback is deeply appreciated.
