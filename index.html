Ready to bring your music into the command line?

Install the package globally via npm.

$ npm install -g mascii

Keep Your Focus

Listen to your local music library directly from your workspace. No window switching, no web browsers eating up your RAM, and absolutely zero distractions while you work.

Stream Instantly

Just press the 'y' key to quickly load online audio streams and YouTube content right into the app, without leaving the terminal environment.

Save Your Memory

Ditch the heavy desktop music applications that lag your workspace. MASCII uses almost nothing, giving you fluid background playback while keeping your system fast.

Total Keyboard Control

Change tracks, pause, adjust the volume, and instantly swap through built-in equalizer music presets using fast, easy-to-remember shortcuts.
