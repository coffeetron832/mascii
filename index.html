Zero Distractions

Stream audio directly inside your current environment. No bulky interfaces, no heavy frameworks, and no graphical overhead keeping you away from your workspace.

Instant Streaming

Press the 'y' key to uncap immediate access to online audio streams and YouTube content, removing browser tabs and keeping your focus locked.

Built for Performance

An ultra-lightweight architecture designed explicitly to deliver fluid sound and zero friction while navigating, scripting, or handling daily layouts.

Uncompromising Sound

Take full control over your media through dedicated equalizer presets, complete keyboard shortcuts, and a highly responsive terminal soundtrack experience.
